# TerraIQ

## Vision

TerraIQ is a Land Intelligence Platform that enables real estate companies to manage land, projects, customers, sales, and geospatial information from one unified system.

It combines GIS, CRM, project management, business intelligence, and land administration into a single enterprise application.

---

## Primary Users

- Real Estate Companies
- Land Surveyors
- Sales Teams
- Property Managers
- Company Directors
- Investors
- Customers
